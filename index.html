Questions, answered

Common things clients ask before we start. Don’t see yours? Send me an email.

What services do you offer?

UI/UX design, website and landing page design, and mobile app design — from wireframes and user flows to polished, developer-ready screens in Figma.

How does your design process work?

We start by understanding your goals and users, then move through wireframes, visual design and a clickable prototype. You review at each step so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A landing page is usually a matter of days, while a full app or multi-page website takes a few weeks. I’ll give you a clear timeline before we begin.

What do I need to get started?

A short brief about your product, your audience and your goals. Brand assets and examples of designs you like are helpful but not required.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Every project includes revision rounds so the final design fits what you need.
